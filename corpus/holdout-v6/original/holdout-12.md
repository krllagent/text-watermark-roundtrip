# Operations Handoff for the Fictional North Room Performance

*Fictional operations handoff: Sable Door Theatre, all names, and all timings are invented.*

The North Room performance runs Friday and Saturday with 96 seats, two entrances, and one 18-minute interval. Doors open at 18:40; the first scene begins at 19:05. This note covers only audience arrival and the interval bar, not stage or fire procedures.

Friday sold 89 tickets. Seventy-four guests arrived before 18:55, and the final 15 arrived within nine minutes. The west entrance processed 6.2 people per minute; the east entrance processed 3.8 because its scanner frequently lost the venue network. Keep both entrances open, but set device `SCAN-E2` to offline mode before doors.

> "A backup is useful only if someone knows when to use it."

For example, ticket SD-441 displayed a blank screen at the east door. The host tried to refresh it three times, creating a two-minute delay. In offline mode, the same ticket can be checked against the retained list in about 12 seconds. Do not photograph a guest’s screen or write their email on paper.

The interval created a different problem. Sixty-one guests entered the bar in the first five minutes, but the card terminal could finish only 28 payments. Because pre-poured water sat beside the payment line, people seeking free water joined the same queue. Move water to the small north table and add a plain sign before Saturday. We expect that change to reduce line length by 20%, although Friday provides no separate count of water-only visitors, so the estimate is uncertain.

The fictional float is £240. The bar lead, @SableDoorOps, must verify it at 18:15 and again after close. If the terminal fails for more than three minutes, use paper order numbers and take payment when service returns; cash is not accepted for this event.

Saturday’s primary checks are entry time, offline lookups, and the longest observed bar line. A faster entrance is not enough if accessibility questions receive a rushed response. Guests who need step-free seating should remain with the front-of-house lead.

The handoff page is https://sabledoor.example/ops/north-room. Urgent fictional questions would go to house@sabledoor.example. Friday attendance was 92.7% of capacity, and reported sales were £1,146.
