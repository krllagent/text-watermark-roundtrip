# Procurement Brief: Rent the Fictional Label Printer Before Buying It

*Fictional procurement brief: Kestrel Pantry, every supplier, and all figures below are invented.*

Recommendation: rent one thermal label printer for eight weeks at $185 per week. Do not purchase the $6,900 unit until the packing team completes the trial.

Kestrel Pantry currently prints 1,400 jar labels each week on office sheets. Staff discard an average of 126 labels because rows shift or ink smears. Reprinting takes about 3.5 labour hours and $74 in materials. The proposed printer cuts individual labels from a roll and uses template `KP-JAR-2`.

> "A lower unit cost matters only if the new routine survives a busy day."

The supplier claims 99.5% readable output, but that number comes from its own controlled demonstration. Our jars vary in temperature and surface moisture. For example, a fictional chilled plum jar developed condensation within four minutes; the sample label adhered at first and lifted at one corner after 40 minutes. A warm dry jar did not show that problem.

The financial case appears strong. If waste fell by 70%, weekly savings would reach roughly $126, including time. That does not make an immediate purchase correct. The estimate values staff time that may shift to other packing work rather than disappear, and rental training could temporarily increase errors. Savings and throughput are related, but neither guarantees the other.

During the trial, one line will use the rental machine and one will keep the existing sheets. Operations will select the lines weekly to avoid assigning every easy product to the new device. This careful method should produce a clear comparison. We will check unreadable labels, reprints, packing minutes per 100 jars, and labels that lift within 24 hours.

Purchase requires fewer than 2% reprints, no more than one missed allergen review, and at least $90 of weekly net savings after rental supplies. A safety-label error ends the trial; a good average cannot cancel that failure. The scope excludes redesigning labels or connecting the printer to inventory software.

The fictional brief is at https://kestrelpantry.example/procurement/labels. Supplier queries would go to buying@kestrelpantry.example, and @KestrelOps records the results. The eight-week rental ceiling is $1,480.
