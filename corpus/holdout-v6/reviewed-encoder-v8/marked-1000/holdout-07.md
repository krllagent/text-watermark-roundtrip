# Policy Note: When Fictional Parcel Notes May Be Machine-Translated

*Fictional policy note: Lantern Post, its staff, and its records are invented for this language test.*

Lantern Post may use machine translation for routine parcel notes only when the original message remains visible and a named dispatcher checks any instruction that could change delivery. This policy begins with a 60-day trial covering Spanish and French messages at the fictional East Yard.

The aim is to reduce waiting, not to remove human judgment. Last quarter, 312 notes waited a median of 27 minutes for bilingual review. A basic translation draft could help dispatchers identify ordinary requests such as “leave with reception.” It must not be used alone for hazardous-goods labels, payment disputes, access codes, or a message whose meaning is unclear.

> "Faster reading is useful only when the route to correction stays open."

For example, a note saying that a customer will be “near the blue gate” can be drafted automatically because the driver can verify the place on arrival. A note that appears to cancel delivery requires a human response. Negation is especially important: missing one “not” could reverse the action.

Every draft will display token `MT_UNVERIFIED` until a dispatcher selects approve, edit, or escalate. Approved notes retain the source, translation, reviewer, and time for 30 days. The service costs a fictional $0.03 per note, with a trial cap of $420. We will not send names, phone numbers, or full addresses to the test system; synthetic placeholders replace them before translation.

Success means at least 95% of eligible notes receive a usable draft within two minutes, while fewer than 2% require correction after approval. These measures are related but not identical. A high approval rate could show accurate drafts, or it could show that busy reviewers accept plausible text too quickly. Therefore, an independent reviewer will inspect a random set of 40 notes each week.

This policy is deliberately narrow. It does not authorize customer chat, legal documents, or new languages. If two severe meaning changes occur, the trial ends even if the aggregate percentage looks good.

The fictional policy is at https://lanternpost.example/policies/translation. Staff questions would go to policy@lanternpost.example; #LanternReview publishes the weekly count. The approved budget remains $420.
