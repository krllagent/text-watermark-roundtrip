# Board Update: Pause the Fictional Expansion, Continue the Evidence Work

*Fictional board update: Rivermark Commons, every location, and all financial data below are invented.*

Recommendation: do not open a third Rivermark Commons study lounge this year. Continue the existing two sites for one quarter while testing whether evening memberships can cover extended hours.

The second site reached 71% desk occupancy between 10:00 and 16:00 during its first 12 weeks. At first glance, that result supports expansion. Yet occupancy after 18:00 was only 29%, and both current leases include unused evening capacity. A third address would add visibility, but it would also add a fictional $118,000 annual commitment before we understand the time pattern.

> "Growth is not the same decision as learning where demand lives."

One example is the fictional Tuesday writing group. Its 14 members meet at 19:00 and fill the quiet room, but only six hold paid memberships. The organiser pays a $45 group fee. Counting every attendee as an occupied desk shows strong use; counting only member revenue shows a weaker commercial result. Neither view is false, and the relation between community activity and future membership remains uncertain.

We propose offer `EVENING-8`: eight weekday evenings for $52, capped at 60 passes across both sites. This basic method should produce a clear result before any lease decision. The trial will cost $3,200 in staffing and utilities. Continue extended hours only if at least 42 passes sell, 70% of buyers attend twice, and incremental revenue covers direct cost by 110%.

This is not a general retreat from expansion. Daytime renewals are 84%, complaints are below 1% of visits, and the first site is profitable under the fictional accounts. The caveat is scope: those signals support the current sites, not a new lease in a different district.

The main risk of waiting is losing a vacant property. That property may be attractive because it is rare, or it may simply create urgency unrelated to customer need. We should not let availability substitute for evidence.

The board should review pass sales, repeat attendance, support load, and contribution margin on 30 November. That check may show a different outcome from occupancy alone. The fictional pack is at https://rivermark.example/board/evening. Questions would go to board@rivermark.example; @RivermarkNumbers will show the 110% threshold.
